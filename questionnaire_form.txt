SEMINAR 2 CLASS QUESTIONNAIRE ============================================================================

Form title:   Twelve quick questions (Social Science Simulations)
Description:  Twelve quick questions, about two minutes, anonymous. There are no right answers;
              some questions are deliberately odd. We will use the class's answers as data in the
              next seminar. Every question is optional.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1  [emp_exp]   Short answer, whole number 0–10
    Think of 10 passengers on a Tartu bus. How many of them, do you think, are travelling
    without a valid ticket?

Q2  [norm_exp]  Short answer, whole number 0–10
    Think of 10 Tartu residents. How many of them would disapprove if they saw someone
    travelling without a ticket?

Q3  [justif]    Linear scale 1–10. Label at 1: Never. Label at 10: Always.
    Do you think travelling without a ticket can be justified?

Q4  [thr_fare]  Short answer, whole number 0–10
    Suppose you knew for certain that a given number out of 10 passengers travel without a
    ticket. At what number would you consider doing it yourself?

Q4b [thr_fare_never]  Checkbox (one box)
    Tick if you would never do it, whatever the number.

Q5  [thr_nbr]   Short answer, whole number 0–8
    Imagine moving to a street of eight households. How many of the eight should be "like you",
    in whatever sense matters to you, for you to feel comfortable there?

Q6  [likert_nbr]  Linear scale 1–5. Label at 1: Strongly disagree. Label at 5: Strongly agree.
    "I would feel uncomfortable if most of my neighbours were different from me."

Q7  [coffee]    Short answer, number
    How many cups of coffee (or tea) do you drink on a typical day?

Q8  [thr_movie] Short answer, whole number 0–10
    Your neighbour likes loud movie nights. How many such nights in one month before you would
    say something?

Q8b [thr_movie_never]  Checkbox (one box)
    Tick if you would never say anything.

Q9  [thr_app]   Short answer, whole number 0–10
    You would start using a new messaging app once how many of your ten closest friends use it?

Q10 [trust_tt]  Linear scale 1–5. Label at 1: Not at all. Label at 5: Completely.
    How much do you trust the Tartu bus timetable?

Q11 [trust_why] Paragraph (free text)
    In one sentence: why?

Q12 [bus_use]   Multiple choice (one answer)
    How often do you use a Tartu bus or a Tartu Smart Bike?
    Options: Never / A few times a year / Monthly / Weekly / Daily
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Why these twelve:
  Q4, Q5, Q8, Q9  the same object four times: a threshold, elicited directly, in four domains
  Q3, Q6, Q10     attitudes on a scale: the number is not the input; a mapping must be chosen
  Q1, Q2          beliefs about others (empirical and normative expectations): what makes a norm
  Q7              a true number, needing no translation: the control
  Q11             text, to be coded into categories (by hand, then with an LLM)
  Q12             an ordered category, to split the class into users and non-users

Q1, Q2 and Q4 are all "out of 10" so that a student's belief about others and their own
threshold sit on the same scale and can be compared in class.

Note: If fewer than ~15 responses arrive, the notebook falls back to
synthetic respondents drawn from the WVS distributions and says so on screen.
