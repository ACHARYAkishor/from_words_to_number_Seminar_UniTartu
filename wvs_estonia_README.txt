wvs_estonia_justifiable.csv
===========================
Published frequency tables (counts of valid cases) for three items of the World Values Survey
"justifiable" battery, Estonia, waves 3 (1996) and 6 (2011). Question: "Please tell me for each of
the following actions whether you think it can always be justified, never be justified, or
something in between", scale 1 = never justifiable ... 10 = always justifiable.

Columns: item, wave, year, WVS variable name, valid n, missing n, counts for scale points 1..10,
and the catalogue page each row was read from (IHSN Survey Catalog, which republishes the WVS
documentation; the same figures are in the WVS online analysis tool at worldvaluessurvey.org).

Citation: Inglehart, R., Haerpfer, C., Moreno, A., Welzel, C., Kizilova, K., Diez-Medrano, J.,
Lagos, M., Norris, P., Ponarin, E. & Puranen, B. (eds.) (2014). World Values Survey: Round Three
(1995-1998) and Round Six (2010-2014), Country-Pooled Datafiles. Madrid: JD Systems Institute.
Estonia 1996: n = 1,021; Estonia 2011: n = 1,533.

Note for the seminar: counts are respondents in the data file, not weighted population figures.
For our purpose (drawing synthetic respondents and translating a scale into model inputs) the
unweighted shares are what we want.
